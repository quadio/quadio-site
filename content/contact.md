Use the form for general work. The browser encrypts the fields to our public key, then posts ciphertext to [form-submit.quad.io](https://form-submit.quad.io). The host stores and emails the ciphertext; it does not see the message.

Hiring still goes to [careers@quad.io](mailto:careers@quad.io).
