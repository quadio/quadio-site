This site is a static brochure. It does not create accounts, run a search index, or collect form submissions.

## What stays on your device

If you choose a color mode, the site stores that preference in your browser's local storage so the next visit can match it. That value does not leave your device.

## Email

If you write to [hello@quad.io](mailto:hello@quad.io) or [careers@quad.io](mailto:careers@quad.io), we receive whatever you put in the message. We use that correspondence to reply and to evaluate applications. We do not sell it.

## Other sites

Links to products such as [Rgit](https://rgit.rs/) leave this site. Those properties have their own practices.
